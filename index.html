Questions

Quick answers.

How long does it take to build my site?

Basic sites usually go live within a week of receiving your content. Premium sites take one to two weeks. Complex sites depend on the features, but we'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo (if you have one), a few photos, and a rough idea of what you want each page to say. Don't have all of that? No problem. We'll help you fill in the gaps.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once it's paid for, the site is yours. We'll connect it to your domain and hand over everything you need.

Can I update the site myself later?

Absolutely. We'll walk you through how to make simple changes, or you can reach out and we'll handle updates for a small fee.

Do you only work with businesses in North Carolina?

North Carolina is our home base and where most of our clients are. If you're elsewhere and like what you see, send a message and we'll talk.

What about hosting and domain costs?

Hosting for most sites is free or very low cost through the platforms we use. Your domain name typically runs about $10 to $20 a year. We'll explain it all in plain English before anything is purchased.
